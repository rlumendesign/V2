Hi, I'm Ronald A. Lalaguna.

RLumen (“Illuminating Brand Identities”) is a dual-capability support studio I founded to streamline operations and elevate the visual presence of growing businesses.

As a General Virtual Assistant and Visual Designer, I provide a hybrid mix of administrative operations and creative support. I am currently refining my technical competencies through Virtual Assistance and ICT skills training programs, and I bring hands-on proficiency in Notion, Trello, Canva, and Figma.

I help busy founders, coaches, and brand owners clear back-end bottlenecks—such as inbox management, calendar scheduling, document organization, and data entry—while creating impactful visual assets that keep their brands polished and consistent.
